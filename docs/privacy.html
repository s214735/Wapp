Wapp privacy

Wapp is a personal app hosted on GitHub Pages. It has no application database or analytics service.

Data on your device

Stretch routines, Counter records, and spreadsheet connection settings are saved in your browser. Backups contain Stretch and Counter data; spreadsheet credentials, links, graph data and access tokens are excluded. GitHub's hosting service receives ordinary website requests and may keep its own access logs.

Google Sheets

When you configure and connect Google Sheets, Google handles sign-in and permission. Wapp requests access to files you select using Google's drive.file permission, which permits reading and editing those files. This version of Wapp only reads chart definitions and the cells used by the selected charts; it does not write to your spreadsheet. Requests go directly from your browser to Google. Graphs are drawn locally with a bundled chart library.

The app keeps access tokens and downloaded chart data in memory only. They are not uploaded to GitHub, included in JSON backups, or stored in the offline app cache. Disconnecting or reloading clears them. Your spreadsheet link and Google connection configuration remain in this browser so you can reconnect. Google scripts are loaded when you open the configured Data page.

Your controls

Keep your spreadsheet private. Use Disconnect to clear the current connection and graph display. Revoke saved Google authorization through Google account connections. Browser website-data deletion removes Wapp's local settings and records, so export any Stretch or Counter data you want to keep first.

For questions about this personal app, contact its repository owner through the Wapp repository. Do not post private spreadsheet data or credentials in public issues.
